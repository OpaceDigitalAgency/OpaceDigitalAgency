## AI content detection and watermarking

| Project | What it provides |
| --- | --- |
| **[Opace AI Content Integrity: Free AI Detector, Invisible Character & Watermark Checker](https://github.com/OpaceDigitalAgency/opace-ai-content-integrity)** | Free, open-source AI content detection that runs in your browser or on our EU server, with no sign-up and no per-check cost. A trained classifier reads the whole document section by section and reports the strongest section rather than an average. Also detects invisible Unicode characters and Cyrillic lookalikes, reads C2PA Content Credentials, and includes a SynthID-Text watermark lab ported from Google DeepMind's reference implementation. Every result names the check that ran, its version and its measured limits, and no score is ever presented as proof of authorship. Ships as a web checker, WordPress plugin, Chrome extension, Astro integration and CLI. |
